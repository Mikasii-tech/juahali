Three steps from symptom to number

  1. 1

    Sign in

    Use your school email. Your name and student ID are already filled in.

  2. 2

    Tell us what is wrong

    Pick a symptom, then answer a few yes or no questions. You never choose your own severity.

  3. 3

    Watch your place move

    See your live position and a wait estimate based on recent consultations.

A queue that cannot be gamed, and never forgets anyone

Questions, not labels

Severity comes from your answers, so nobody can jump the line by picking a scarier word.

Red flags go first

A warning-sign answer makes the case critical straight away.

Waiting counts too

The longer you wait, the higher you climb. After a set time you are called next, so nobody is stuck forever.

Every step is logged

Each status change records who made it and when.

Minor symptom? We may suggest the campus pharmacy to save you a wait. It is only a suggestion, and you can always join the doctor's queue.

One system, four views

Patient

Check in, follow live status and review your visits.

Receptionist

Runs the live queue and calls patients. Sees only the last visit date.

Doctor or nurse

Sees the next patient, full history and adds consultation notes.

Admin

Manages staff accounts and reads reports on visits and waiting times.
